# Phac do su dung to yen theo muc tieu suc khoe trong 12 tuan

To yen la thuc pham gia tri cao, nhung hieu qua khong den tu viec "an that nhieu", ma den tu **lieu luong deu**, **thoi diem dung**, va **muc tieu ro rang**. Bai viet nay trinh bay phac do 12 tuan co the ap dung cho nguoi ban ron, nguoi can phuc hoi, va nguoi muon toi uu suc khoe nen tang.

## 1) Nguyen tac xay phac do

1. Dung deu hon dung don dot.
2. Uu tien chat luong nguyen lieu va ky thuat chung.
3. Can doi voi tong khau phan (dam, rau, nuoc, giac ngu).
4. Theo doi dau hieu co the moi 2 tuan de dieu chinh.

## 2) Chia 3 giai doan trong 12 tuan

## Giai doan A (Tuan 1-4): Thich nghi va on dinh tieu hoa

- Tan suat: 3 lan/tuan.
- Khau phan moi lan: 3-5g to yen kho.
- Muc tieu: de co the lam quen, giam day bung, theo doi giac ngu.

**Goi y thoi diem**
- Sang som sau khi uong nuoc am 20-30 phut.
- Hoac buoi toi truoc khi ngu 60-90 phut.

## Giai doan B (Tuan 5-8): Tang cuong theo muc tieu

- Tan suat: 4 lan/tuan.
- Khau phan moi lan: 5-7g to yen kho.
- Muc tieu: cai thien phuc hoi, tap trung tinh than, da va giac ngu.

**Mau lich de ap dung**
- Thu 2, 4, 6, Chu nhat: dung yen.
- Thu 3, 5, 7: uu tien bua an giau dam + rau xanh.

## Giai doan C (Tuan 9-12): Duy tri ben vung

- Tan suat: 3-4 lan/tuan.
- Khau phan moi lan: 4-6g to yen kho.
- Muc tieu: giu nen tang suc khoe, duy tri hieu qua da dat duoc.

## 3) Bien the theo doi tuong

## Nguoi can phuc hoi sau om/day viec cao

- Tuan 1-2: 4 lan/tuan, 4-5g.
- Tuan 3-8: 4-5 lan/tuan, 5-7g.
- Tuan 9-12: 3-4 lan/tuan, 5-6g.

## Nguoi lon tuoi

- Uu tien tan suat deu, khau phan vua.
- 3-4 lan/tuan, 4-6g; ket hop di bo nhe va ngu som.

## Nguoi can toi uu tri nao - giac ngu

- Uu tien dung buoi toi.
- 3-4 lan/tuan, 4-6g, theo doi chat luong ngu va do tap trung ban ngay.

## 4) KPI theo doi moi 2 tuan

Ban co the dung bang don gian:

| Chi so | Tuan 0 | Tuan 2 | Tuan 4 | Tuan 8 | Tuan 12 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| So gio ngu trung binh |  |  |  |  |  |
| Muc do met moi (1-10) |  |  |  |  |  |
| Do on dinh tieu hoa (1-10) |  |  |  |  |  |
| Do tap trung (1-10) |  |  |  |  |  |

Neu sau 4 tuan khong thay doi, nguyen nhan thuong la:
- Dung khong deu.
- Chua toi uu giac ngu.
- Khau phan tong the mat can doi.

## 5) 5 loi pho bien khien phac do that bai

1. Tang lieu qua nhanh ngay tu tuan dau.
2. Dung yen thay the bua an chinh.
3. Chung qua lau lam mat mui va giam cam quan.
4. Bo qua nuoc uong va chat xo.
5. Khong ghi nhan thay doi de dieu chinh.

## 6) Cong thuc chung co ban de giu chat luong

- Ngam yen 20-40 phut tuy loai.
- Chung cach thuy 20-30 phut o nhiet vua.
- Neu them duong phen/tao do, chi them cuoi qua trinh.

## Ket luan

Phac do yen hieu qua nhat la phac do ban co the duy tri lau dai. Bat dau tu muc vua phai, do ket qua theo chu ky 2 tuan, va dieu chinh theo muc tieu ca nhan. Day la cach bien yen sao thanh mot phan cua he thong cham soc suc khoe ben vung, thay vi mot giai phap ngan han.
